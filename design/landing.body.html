FAQ

Questions, answered

Is FXcrypt custodial?

No. FXcrypt is fully non-custodial. Your private keys are encrypted on your own device and are only ever decrypted locally to sign a transaction you've approved. We never hold your funds or your recovery phrase.

Which chains and exchanges are supported?

On-chain trading spans Solana, Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Polygon and Arbitrum via their leading DEXs. CEX signals and auto-execute connect to Binance, Bybit and MEXC through read-and-trade API keys.

Does Pointer trade automatically?

Only if you tell it to. By default Pointer prepares a trade proposal — token, size, slippage, safety checks — and waits for your approval. Auto-execute and copy-trading are opt-in features you control per rule.

What does it cost?

The Free tier is free forever with a 1.0% swap fee. Pro is $29/mo (0.5% fee) and Elite is $99/mo (0.2% fee), each unlocking more automation, AI usage and wallets. Annual billing gives you two months free.

Is this financial advice?

No. FXcrypt provides tools, data and automation — not financial advice. Crypto trading carries significant risk, including the loss of your entire capital. Always do your own research.
